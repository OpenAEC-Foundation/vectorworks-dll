# Notice

This library is an independent implementation that reads Vectorworks (.vwx) files. It exists for
interoperability: so that people can open their own building and engineering data without the
program that wrote it.

## How it was made

- Written from scratch, under the clean-room rules in the README.
- The format was learned by observing files: files we made ourselves, or files we are allowed to
  use. No vendor binaries were decompiled or disassembled, and no SDK headers, leaked or licensed
  documentation or third-party parser code were copied.
- The repository holds no model files of clients or other third parties.

## Legal basis

In the European Union, a lawful user may observe, study and test a program to understand the
ideas behind it, and may decompile it where that is needed for interoperability (Directive
2009/24/EC, Articles 5(3) and 6). Contract terms that forbid this are void (Article 8). The Court
of Justice of the EU held that data file formats are not protected by copyright as the expression
of a computer program (SAS Institute v World Programming, C-406/10, 2012).
